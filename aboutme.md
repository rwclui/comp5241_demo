# About me

Dr. Richard Lui is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). He obtained his Bachelor of Science, Master of Philosophy, and Doctor of Philosophy degrees in Computer Science from The University of Hong Kong. Before joining PolyU, he worked as an instructor in the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong. [polyu.edu](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/comp/people/academic-staff/dr-lui-wing-cheung-richard/)

At PolyU, Dr. Lui is recognised for his commitment to teaching and educational innovation. His work focuses on e-learning, simulation games, gamification, and the development of interactive learning platforms. He has also served as the principal investigator of various University Grants Committee-funded projects since 2013, demonstrating his active contribution to teaching research and technology-enhanced education. [polyu.edu](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/comp/people/academic-staff/dr-lui-wing-cheung-richard/)

# My achievement

Dr. Lui’s achievements include receiving several PolyU Faculty Awards in Teaching, including an Outstanding Award as an individual in 2016 and team awards in 2022 and 2023. Through the use of simulation games and innovative pedagogy, he aims to make learning more engaging, practical, and relevant to students. His work reflects the growing role of digital technologies in improving higher education. [polyu.edu](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/comp/people/academic-staff/dr-lui-wing-cheung-richard/)
